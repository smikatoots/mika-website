To humanize AI text, give the AI real examples of your writing and run every draft through a focused editing pass. Those two settings get my scripts, emails, and DMs much closer to my voice before I do the final read-through myself.

I use AI for first drafts because it saves me time. But I do not ask the model to guess what "sounds like Mika." I give it the evidence and editing rules it needs to get most of the way there.

## How do you humanize AI text without rewriting it yourself?

Use two editing skills after the first draft, save strong examples of your own writing beside those instructions, then compare drafting tools with the same input. A skill is a saved set of instructions your AI can run on command. If that term is new to you, read [What is a Skill?](/ai/what-is-a-skill) first.

**1. Install two editing skills.** I use [Humanizer](/ai/humanizer) and Peter Yang's [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop). Humanizer scans for broad AI writing patterns and protects the core meaning. No AI Slop catches habits such as binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, fake insight setups, and robotic rhythm without polishing away your personality.

![Humanizer skill instructions for removing AI writing patterns while preserving meaning, voice, and personality](/ai-guides/how-to-humanize-ai-text-humanizer-skill.png "Humanizer gives the AI a concrete editing job: find the patterns, rewrite them, and preserve the writer's meaning and voice.")

You do not need to memorize every AI tell yourself. The skill keeps the checklist, so you can focus on whether the result sounds right.

**2. Draft first, then edit.** Write the first draft with your normal brief, then run Humanizer and No AI Slop as separate passes. Review the changes after each one. I do not combine every instruction into one giant prompt because I want to see what the editor changed and undo anything that feels too polished.

**3. Save your own writing as the example.** Inside the skill folder, keep the instructions in `SKILL.md` and your strongest past work in a separate file such as `examples.md`. Use scripts for a script-writing skill, emails for an email skill, and DMs for a message-writing skill. The AI can now study your recurring sentence shapes, vocabulary, humor, and level of polish instead of inventing a generic voice profile.

![AI skill folder structure with SKILL.md for instructions and examples.md for past hooks, scripts, and text that performed well](/ai-guides/how-to-humanize-ai-text-writing-examples-folder.png "Keep the editing rules in SKILL.md and real examples of your writing in examples.md.")

If you want to build that folder from scratch, follow the [custom AI skill guide](/ai/how-to-create-custom-skill). The same principle applies beyond writing: durable examples make your instructions more useful because the AI can see the standard you mean.

> **Key insight:** Rules remove the obvious AI tells. Your examples are what teach the AI to sound like you.

**4. Tell the editor what it must preserve.** Ask it to keep your point, specific facts, jokes, blunt language, uncertainty, and any line that already sounds like you. Humanizing a draft should not mean making every paragraph equally smooth. The goal is a cleaner version of your voice, not a generic professional voice.

**5. Compare drafting environments with the same brief.** I moved my own drafting from ChatGPT into Codex because the current model in my setup gives me drafts that need less rewriting. Your results may differ. Paste the same brief and examples into the tools you already have, then keep the one that produces the closest first draft.

**6. Do one final human read.** Read the result out loud and fix anything you would never say. Check names, numbers, links, and claims yourself. If I would not say the sentence out loud, it does not ship.

## What should you put in your writing examples file?

Choose three to five finished pieces that sound unmistakably like you. A smaller set of strong, relevant examples beats a giant archive of mixed work.

For each writing workflow:

1. Keep the examples in the same format you want the AI to produce.
2. Remove drafts you no longer like so old habits do not become instructions.
3. Include enough variety to show your range without mixing completely different voices.
4. Update the file when you publish something that feels more current or more you.

If you work across several projects, pair those examples with [persistent AI context](/ai/how-to-give-claude-memory) so the tool also knows your audience, business, and standing preferences.
