If you post consistently on Instagram, you already have more strategy data than you think.

My husband and I built **InstaSights** so creators can actually use that data instead of guessing what to post next. It helps you analyze your Instagram analytics and hook performance directly inside Claude, then reuse what works in your next post.

You can check it out here: [kingscrosslabs.com/instasights](https://kingscrosslabs.com/instasights).

![InstaSights landing page](/ai-guides/instasights-step-1-hero.png "InstaSights by King's Cross Labs")

## Why this matters

Most creators look at vanity metrics, then move on. InstaSights helps you go one level deeper so you can see which hooks and content patterns are actually worth repeating.

![InstaSights examples and questions](/ai-guides/instasights-step-3-examples.png "Examples of questions you can ask")
![InstaSights included data and transcription](/ai-guides/instasights-step-4-included-data.png "What InstaSights includes")

## Where to start

1. Go to [kingscrosslabs.com/instasights](https://kingscrosslabs.com/instasights) and download the skill.
2. Connect your Instagram account and sync your data.
3. Ask Claude to surface your top-performing hooks, then reuse those patterns in your next posts.
