I wanted a haircut analysis that was actually personalized, not generic hairstyle inspo.

So I asked ChatGPT to evaluate my actual features and build a visual haircut guide with flattering options, avoid lists, and salon-friendly language I can use in a real appointment.

The key was forcing it to explain why each cut works, then narrowing to a top 3 I can act on.

When the prompt is specific, the recommendations are easier to trust and use.

## Prompt to use

```text
I’m uploading a photo of myself. Analyze my face shape, facial proportions, hairline, forehead, jawline, cheekbones, neck length, and overall features to recommend flattering hairstyles.

Create a visual hairstyle guide for me with:

1. **Best haircut styles for me**

   * Show several hairstyle options that would likely suit my face and features.
   * Include different lengths, layers, bangs/fringe options, parting styles, and styling textures.
   * Label each style clearly.

2. **Haircuts or styling choices I should avoid**

   * Show examples of cuts that may be less flattering on me.
   * Explain what they emphasize or why they may not balance my features well.

3. **Why each style works or doesn’t**

   * Give concise notes on how each haircut interacts with my face shape, jawline, forehead, cheekbones, and overall proportions.
   * Include practical salon-friendly language I can show to a hairstylist.

4. **Final recommendation**

   * Pick the top 3 haircut directions for me.
   * Include a short “ask your stylist for…” description for each.

Please keep the output realistic and personalized to my actual face, not generic model hairstyles. Use my uploaded photo as the reference, preserve my identity, and show the hairstyles in a clean visual comparison layout.
```
