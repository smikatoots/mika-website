Creators lose filming hours to insights, stale Saves, unanswered DMs, and media kits. Put Meta Muse on those jobs. It is Meta's personal AI agent. It does the work, and it can keep going after you close the app.

I have not run these Muse workflows for months. Muse just launched. These are the jobs I would give it so I stay on camera.

If you'd rather save these for later, I put the prompts in a [carousel on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdkNF27j-vw/) you can swipe through.

## What can content creators do with Meta Muse?

Muse sits inside Instagram and Facebook, so it can see insights, DMs, Saves, comments, and brand inbound. It runs on a Muse Secure VM, a private computer with its own browser. That is [agentic AI](/ai/chat-based-ai-vs-agentic-ai) for a creator: the brief can finish while the camera is rolling, and Muse asks before it sends or buys anything. You choose which apps it can use.

**1. The Instagram Co-Pilot**

Meta Muse can open Instagram insights, rank the posts that earned saves and shares, and spell out which hooks and formats actually worked. The brief can finish in the background so filming time is not spent inside analytics.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Study my Instagram insights for the last [90 days].
Rank my top 10 posts by saves, shares, and average watch time.
For each, name the hook, format, and why it likely worked.
List 5 patterns I should repeat this week and 3 I should stop.
Save the brief so I can film from it, not from a spreadsheet.
```

**2. The Saves to Reels Pipeline**

Meta Muse can go through a stale Instagram Saves list and turn the keepers into Reel or feed-post ideas, with a hook and a shot outline for each. Meta's own example of this motion is a saved recipe Reel becoming a grocery list. Same idea, pointed at the next post.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Open my Instagram Saves from the last [6 months].
Ignore anything off-niche for [your niche].
Turn the 15 strongest saves into Reel or feed-post ideas.
For each, give a hook, a 3-shot outline, and a caption draft.
Flag which saves are stale and not worth making.
```

**3. The Repurposing Machine**

Meta Muse can take one talking-head video and turn it into a Reel, a carousel, a week of Stories, and a Threads post, each cut and captioned for the format. A carousel is a multi-slide post people swipe through. It waits for approval before anything publishes.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Take this talking-head video: [link or file].
Cut a Reel, a 7-slide carousel, 5 Stories, and one Threads post.
Write a caption and on-screen text for each format.
Keep my voice from these example posts: [paste 3 captions].
Show me the Reel cut first. Wait for my approval before the rest.
```

**4. The Caption Ghostwriter**

Meta Muse can study past posts until the drafts sound like the account on a good day: the hooks, the cadence, the hashtags. You get a first draft on the screen instead of a blank caption box.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Study my last [40] Instagram posts and learn my voice.
Write 5 caption options for this post: [topic or draft].
Lead with a hook, keep my cadence, suggest 8 hashtags I actually use.
Do not post. Show me the options and wait for approval.
Once I pick one, finalize it for Instagram.
```

**5. The Trend Radar**

Meta Muse can run a weekly scan of creators in the same niche: formats popping off, audios trending, and one thing to try next. The output is a short brief, not forty open tabs.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Once a week, scan these creators: [list 8-12 handles in your niche].
Report formats popping off, audios circulating, and hooks that keep showing up.
Give me one thing to try next week that still sounds like me.
Keep it to one page. No 40-tab dump.
```

**6. The Media Kit on Demand**

Meta Muse can take a brand conversation in DMs, pull the latest Instagram insights, and spin up a media kit with current stats. A media kit is the one-pager brands ask for: audience size, engagement, demographics, past work, and how to buy a post.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
A brand just DMed me: [paste or point Muse at the thread].
Pull my latest Instagram insights: followers, avg reach, saves, and best posts.
Build a one-page media kit for [your niche] with those stats and 4 proof posts.
Include my rates for [Reel / carousel / Stories] as [your rates or "TBD"].
Do not send it. Show me the draft and wait for approval.
```

**7. The 30-Day Content Calendar**

Meta Muse can take a niche and plan 30 days of posts with hooks, formats, and CTAs, then draft each one as it gets approved. A CTA is the line that tells someone what to do next. The calendar lives as a working list, not a spreadsheet that dies in week two.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Plan 30 days of Instagram content for [your niche] and audience [who they are].
Mix Reels, carousels, and Stories. Give each day a hook, format, and CTA.
Draft day 1 now. Stop and wait for my approval before drafting day 2.
When I approve, move to the next day. Keep a running calendar I can film from.
```

**8. The Collab Inbox Triage**

Meta Muse can triage Instagram DMs daily, separate fan chat from anything that involves money, and surface the brand and collab threads first. It can flag rates, deliverables, and deadlines sitting in a buried conversation. Nothing sends until you say so.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Triage my Instagram DMs from the last [24 hours].
Separate fan chat from anything that involves money, rates, gifted product, or a collab.
Surface the money threads first with: who, ask, deadline, and a suggested reply.
Do not send any reply. Wait for my approval on the ones I flag.
```

> **Key insight:** Hand Muse the work that is repetitive, lives in Instagram data, or can run while filming. Keep the hours that belong on camera.
