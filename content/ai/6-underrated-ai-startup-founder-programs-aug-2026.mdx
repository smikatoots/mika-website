YC closed July 27. South Park Commons closed August 2. Entrepreneur First London closed August 4. If you missed those, you are not out of options. Six underrated founder programs are still open, and barely anyone is talking about them.

## What underrated startup programs are still open?

Ordered by urgency. Arc closes first.

### 1. Sequoia Arc — closes August 17

Sequoia's own 7-week program for pre-seed and seed founders.

- **Check size:** $500K–$1M
- **Who it's for:** Pre-seed / seed founders in the Americas + Europe
- **Why it matters:** Open call, so no warm intro needed
- **[Apply here](https://sequoiacap.com/arc)**

### 2. Character Cap Labs — closes September 23

A 5-week sprint built entirely around finding product-market fit.

- **Check size:** $200K for 5%
- **Who it's for:** Founders who need a focused PMF sprint, not a long accelerator tour
- **[Apply here](https://www.character.vc/labs)**

### 3. Solo Founders Cohort 5 — apps open now, starts September 10

Built only for solo founders. Three months in San Francisco.

- **Check size:** $100K for 2.5%
- **Who it's for:** Solo founders who want a cohort designed around building alone
- **[Apply here](https://solofounders.com/)**

### 4. Greylock Edge — rolling

A 3-month build program for pre-idea through seed.

- **Extras:** $500K+ in cloud credits
- **Who it's for:** Founders still early, including pre-idea
- **[Apply here](https://greylock.com/edge/)**

### 5. Founders Inc — rolling

First checks plus a real SF campus with a hardware lab.

- **Check size:** $100K–$250K first checks
- **Campus:** 42,000 sq ft in San Francisco, including a hardware lab
- **Who it's for:** Founders who want capital and a place to build
- **[Apply here](https://f.inc/)**

### 6. 1517 Fund — rolling

Backs dropouts, students, and deep-tech scientists. No degree required.

- **Entry point:** $1K+ micro-grants to get started, plus pre-seed checks
- **Who it's for:** Students, dropouts, and deep-tech builders without the usual credentials
- **[Apply here](https://www.1517fund.com/)**
