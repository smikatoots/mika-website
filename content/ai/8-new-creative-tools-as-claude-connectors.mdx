Most creative software has a learning curve that stops people before they ever start. You've been meaning to try Blender or get into Premiere for months. Between the tutorials and the interface, it never happens.

Anthropic just released connectors for 7 creative tools. The setup is a few clicks. The skill wall just got a lot shorter.

---

These aren't plugins that require a developer. They're official connectors built by Anthropic that let Claude communicate directly with the software. You describe what you want in plain English inside Claude, and Claude talks to the tool for you. If you've avoided creative software because of the complexity, this is for you.

---

### The tools now connected to Claude

**1. Tool: Ableton**

One of the most widely used digital audio workstations. Claude can now interact with it directly, which helps producers automate repetitive parts of a session without losing flow.

[Open Ableton connector](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/ant.dir.gh.ableton.ableton-knowledge)

**2. Tool: Adobe Creative Cloud**

This covers Photoshop, Premiere, Express, and 50+ apps across the Creative Cloud suite. You describe what you want in plain English, and Claude executes it inside Adobe tools.

[Open Adobe connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/adobe-creativity)

**3. Tool: Blender**

Blender is free, powerful, and notoriously hard to learn from scratch. With the connector, you describe a 3D model in plain English and Claude builds the first version for you.

[Open Blender connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/ant.dir.gh.blender.blender-mcp)

**4. Tool: Canva**

Canva gives you fast design workflows for social, slides, and marketing assets. With the connector, you can search, create, and export Canva designs through Claude.

[Open Canva connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/ant.dir.gh.canva.affinity)

**5. Tool: Resolume Arena**

Resolume Arena is used for live visuals and performance setups. The connector helps you work faster on visual directions, cues, and creative iteration.

[Open Resolume Arena connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/ant.dir.gh.resolume.resolume-arena)

**6. Tool: SketchUp**

SketchUp is used by architects, interior designers, and furniture designers. Describe a layout or concept and Claude helps create a starting model so you are not beginning from a blank canvas.

[Open SketchUp connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/sketchup)

**7. Tool: Splice**

Splice is where music producers find royalty-free samples. With the connector, you can search Splice from inside Claude without switching contexts.

[Open Splice connector](https://claude.ai/redirect/website.v1.2cffd3ee-72d3-4b28-a747-43e5da29c077/directory/connectors/splice)

---

### How to connect a tool

Go to Claude.
Click `Customize` in the top right.
Open `Connectors`.
Click `Browse Connectors`.
Search for your tool and click `Add`.
