The average couple spends $4,500 on a wedding planner. I replaced mine with one Claude skill that builds the entire 12-month timeline and drops every due date into our shared Google Calendar in under 60 seconds.

This is **Part 2 of my 10-part AI Wedding Planning series**. [Part 1](/ai/ai-wedding-planning-part-1) was the overview. This is the move that actually saves you the $4K.

## Why this matters

A wedding planner's whole job for the first six months is one thing: the timeline. When to book the venue. When to send save-the-dates. When to do the tasting. When final headcount is due. That is the deliverable you are paying thousands for.

You can build a Claude skill that does exactly that. It asks a few clarifying questions, builds a full 12-month plan sequenced correctly so you are not booking your photographer two weeks before the venue, then pushes every deadline into a shared Google Calendar or Notion with owners and reminders already assigned. No more "I thought you booked the florist."

> **Key insight:** You are not replacing taste or relationships. You are replacing the organizational grunt work that eats your weekends and costs $4,000 when someone else does it.

## Before you run the skill

**Step 0: Connect Google Calendar or Notion**

The skill can only push deadlines somewhere if Claude can reach your tools. Do this once before you run anything.

1. Open **Claude Cowork** on desktop.
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors** (or **Integrations**).
3. Connect **Google Calendar** and/or **Notion**. Pick whichever you and your partner will actually check.
4. For Google Calendar: create or pick a shared calendar and note the exact name. You will paste it into the skill template as `[GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME]`.
5. For Notion: create a blank page or database you want the timeline to live in. Copy the page URL. You will paste it into the skill template as `[NOTION_PAGE_URL]`.

If you have not connected apps in Cowork before, [my 10-minute Claude setup guide](/ai/how-to-setup-claude) walks through the integrations panel step by step.

Once connected, you are done with setup. You will not need to do this again.

## Run the wedding timeline skill

**Step 1: Install the skill and fill in your placeholders**

Copy the template at the bottom of this guide. **Before you install it**, find the `[BRACKET]` fields and replace them with your details: where to sync (Google Calendar or Notion), your shared calendar name, your Notion page URL, partner names, and default owner for vendor tasks.

Then paste it into Claude:

*"Use /skill-creator to turn the template below into a wedding-timeline-builder skill. Keep the trigger description broad so it fires when I mention wedding timelines, deadlines, or planning checklists."*

Or save it as `SKILL.md` inside a folder called `wedding-timeline-builder` and upload it: **Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload**.

**Step 2: Run the skill**

Open a new Cowork session and say something like:

*"Build my wedding timeline."*

The skill will ask you clarifying questions if it needs more info: wedding date, destination or local, guest count, and anything else that would change the timeline. You do not need to front-load every detail. Just answer what it asks.

**Step 3: Review with your partner**

Skim the timeline summary before it syncs. Look for anything specific to your wedding: multi-day events, cultural traditions, a courthouse ceremony, extra travel lead time for guests. Tweak deadlines if something feels off. Five minutes now saves a fight at month eight.

Once you approve, the skill pushes every deadline to your calendar or Notion with owners and reminders already assigned by category. No manual tagging after the fact.

name: wedding-timeline-builder
description: Builds a 12-month wedding planning timeline and syncs every deadline to Google Calendar or Notion. # Wedding Timeline Builder

Build a complete 12-month wedding planning timeline, assign owners and reminders by task category, then push every deadline to Google Calendar or Notion.

## User Configuration (replace before installing)

The person installing this skill must replace every `[BRACKET]` below with their own details:

| Placeholder | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| `[SYNC_DESTINATION]` | `google_calendar`, `notion`, or `ask_every_time` |
| `[GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME]` | Name of your shared calendar (e.g. `Wedding Planning`) |
| `[NOTION_PAGE_URL]` | Full URL of the Notion page or database for tasks |
| `[PARTNER_1_NAME]` | First partner's name (e.g. `Mika`) — used for owner tags |
| `[PARTNER_2_NAME]` | Second partner's name (e.g. `Nick`) — used for owner tags |
| `[DEFAULT_VENDOR_OWNER]` | Who usually handles vendor outreach: `[PARTNER_1_NAME]`, `[PARTNER_2_NAME]`, or `Both` |

If `[SYNC_DESTINATION]` is `ask_every_time`, use AskUserQuestion to let the user pick Google Calendar or Notion at runtime.

## Before You Start

1. **Check connectors first.** The user must have Google Calendar and/or Notion connected in Claude Cowork. If neither is connected, stop and tell them:
   - Open Claude Cowork → Settings → Connectors
   - Connect Google Calendar and/or Notion
   - For Calendar: create or pick a shared calendar named `[GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME]`
   - For Notion: create a page or database at `[NOTION_PAGE_URL]`
   - Do not build the timeline until the chosen destination is connected.

2. **Ask clarifying questions before building.** Use AskUserQuestion whenever anything is missing or ambiguous. Do not guess. At minimum, confirm:
   - Wedding date (exact date or target month)
   - Destination or local (city/region; note if multi-day or international)
   - Guest count (exact number or range)

   Also ask if the answer would change the timeline:
   - Ceremony type (church, courthouse, outdoor, multi-faith, etc.)
   - Already-booked vendors (skip or mark complete)
   - Multi-day events or cultural traditions that need extra tasks
   - Whether `[SYNC_DESTINATION]` is set to `ask_every_time` — if so, ask Google Calendar or Notion now

   If the user gives partial info ("we're getting married next fall in Mexico"), ask follow-up questions until you have enough to sequence deadlines correctly.

## Timeline Rules

- Work **backwards from the wedding date**. Every deadline is a specific date, not "month 8."
- **Sequence dependencies correctly.** Venue before photographer. Save-the-dates before invitations. Tasting before final menu. Never put dependent tasks after their prerequisites.
- **Adjust lead times** based on inputs:
  - **Destination or international:** add 2–4 extra weeks for guest travel info, hotel blocks, and vendor research
  - **150+ guests:** earlier venue booking, earlier RSVP deadline, earlier seating chart
  - **Under 50 guests:** some tasks can move later; still keep legal and vendor lead times
  - **Short timeline (under 9 months):** flag compressed tasks and mark anything at risk in bold
- Base the structure on standard wedding planning milestones. Merge duplicates. Do not invent fake vendor categories.

## Standard Milestones (adapt dates to the wedding)

Use these categories. Shift dates based on the wedding date and inputs above.

| Window | Tasks |
| --- | --- |
| 12 months out | Set budget · Draft guest list · Define wedding style · Research venues · Book venue |
| 11 months out | Book photographer/videographer · Book caterer (if separate) · Start attire shopping |
| 10 months out | Book DJ or band · Book florist · Book officiant · Reserve hotel room blocks |
| 9 months out | Order save-the-dates · Book hair and makeup · Book transportation/shuttles |
| 8 months out | Send save-the-dates · Order invitations · Start honeymoon research |
| 7 months out | Schedule cake/dessert tasting · Book rehearsal dinner venue · Set up registry |
| 6 months out | Attire ordered (allow time for alterations) · Book rentals · Schedule menu tasting |
| 5 months out | Mail invitations · RSVP deadline set · Book day-of coordinator if needed |
| 4 months out | Finalize flowers · Hair/makeup trial · Start writing vows/ceremony |
| 3 months out | Track RSVPs · Start seating chart · Confirm all vendor contracts |
| 2 months out | Final headcount to caterer · Apply for marriage license (check local rules) · Vendor final payments |
| 1 month out | Final attire fitting · Confirm day-of timeline with vendors · Rehearsal dinner details |
| 2 weeks out | Finalize seating chart · Confirm guest count · Marriage license in hand (if required before ceremony) |
| 1 week out | Confirm delivery and arrival times · Pack emergency kit · Share day-of contact list |

Add destination-specific tasks when relevant: guest travel guide, visa/passport reminders, welcome event, post-wedding brunch.

## Owner Assignment (by category)

Assign an owner to every task using `[PARTNER_1_NAME]`, `[PARTNER_2_NAME]`, or `Both`. Use these defaults unless the user specifies otherwise during clarifying questions:

| Category | Default owner | Example tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Venue & vendors** | `[DEFAULT_VENDOR_OWNER]` | Book venue, photographer, caterer, florist, DJ, officiant |
| **Guest communications** | `Both` | Save-the-dates, invitations, RSVP tracking, seating chart |
| **Attire & beauty** | `[PARTNER_1_NAME]` | Dress/suit shopping, fittings, hair/makeup trials |
| **Budget & registry** | `Both` | Set budget, set up registry, track payments |
| **Travel & logistics** | `[DEFAULT_VENDOR_OWNER]` | Hotel blocks, transportation, guest travel guide |
| **Ceremony & vows** | `Both` | Write vows, ceremony details, rehearsal dinner |
| **Legal & admin** | `Both` | Marriage license, final headcount, vendor contracts |

If the user says one partner is handling a specific area (e.g. "Nick is doing all vendor calls"), override the defaults for that category.

## Reminder Rules (by category)

Set calendar reminders automatically when syncing. Do not ask the user to set these manually.

| Category | Reminder timing |
| --- | --- |
| **Major vendor bookings** (venue, photographer, caterer) | 2 weeks before due date |
| **Guest communications** (save-the-dates, invitations, RSVP deadline) | 1 week before |
| **Mid-priority tasks** (attire, tastings, trials, rentals) | 1 week before |
| **Final-month tasks** (license, headcount, seating chart) | 3 days before |
| **Week-of tasks** (confirmations, emergency kit, contact list) | 1 day before |

## Output Format

### Phase 1: Show the timeline (before syncing)

Present a scannable summary grouped by month. For each task include:
- **Task name**
- **Due date** (specific calendar date)
- **Category** (from the tables above)
- **Owner** (`[PARTNER_1_NAME]`, `[PARTNER_2_NAME]`, or `Both`)
- **Reminder** (from reminder rules above)
- **Notes** (one line only if needed)

End with a short "at risk" list if the timeline is compressed.

Ask: *"Ready for me to push these to [GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME / Notion]? Every task will include an owner and a reminder."*

Wait for explicit approval before syncing.

### Phase 2: Sync to Google Calendar

If `[SYNC_DESTINATION]` is `google_calendar` (or user chose Calendar), create events on `[GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME]`:

- **Title:** `[Wedding] Task name` (e.g. `[Wedding] Book photographer`)
- **Date:** the due date (all-day event is fine)
- **Description:** `Owner: [name] · Category: [category] · [one-line note if needed]`
- **Reminder:** per the reminder rules table above

After creating events, share a count: *"Created X deadlines on [GOOGLE_CALENDAR_NAME], each with an owner and reminder."*

### Phase 3: Sync to Notion

If `[SYNC_DESTINATION]` is `notion` (or user chose Notion), create one row/item per task on `[NOTION_PAGE_URL]`:

- Task name
- Due date
- Category
- Owner (`[PARTNER_1_NAME]`, `[PARTNER_2_NAME]`, or `Both`)
- Reminder (date, calculated from reminder rules)
- Status: Not started
- Notes (optional)

If no database exists, create a simple table on the page they provided.

After syncing, share the Notion page link back to the user.

## Quality Checklist (run silently before presenting)

- [ ] Every task has a specific date, not just a month label
- [ ] Venue is booked before dependent vendors (photographer, caterer, florist)
- [ ] Save-the-dates go out before invitations
- [ ] RSVP deadline is at least 6–8 weeks before the wedding
- [ ] Marriage license timing matches the wedding location (flag if unsure)
- [ ] Destination weddings include travel and hotel-block tasks
- [ ] No duplicate tasks
- [ ] Every task has an owner assigned by category
- [ ] Every task has a reminder set by category
- [ ] User approved before anything was pushed to Calendar or Notion

## Tone

Direct and warm. These users are stressed and non-technical. No jargon. If they seem overwhelmed, offer to start with just the next 90 days instead of the full 12 months.
