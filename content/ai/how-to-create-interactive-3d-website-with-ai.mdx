You can add an interactive 3D element to your website without writing code: start from a 3D component that comes with its source code, and have Claude Code rebuild it in your own brand. That's how my press page became a shelf of books you can pull off and flip through, in my colors and my fonts, after one prompt and a few rounds of feedback. Every article, podcast, and feature I've been in is now a page inside a book.

My old press page was a grid of 36 thumbnails nobody clicked. Interactive 3D sites usually mean hiring a developer or settling for a template that looks like everyone else's. The shortcut is to borrow the *interaction* from something you love and let AI rebuild the *look* from your own brand rules.

![Mika Reyes' press page as a 3D bookshelf of five branded volumes in red, green, yellow, charcoal, and aqua on a charcoal shelf](/ai-guides/how-to-create-interactive-3d-website-with-ai-shelf.png "The finished press page: five volumes, one per chapter of my press.")

## How do you create a 3D interaction for your website?

You give the AI two inputs: the 3D component's source code for how it *moves*, and your brand file for how it *looks*. Claude Code combines them, and you refine the result by testing it in your browser and giving feedback in plain English.

### Set it up

**1. Find a component you can't stop playing with.** I found mine on [ThreeUI](https://threeui.com), a library of interactive 3D components. Look for one that comes with its source code or a "copy prompt" button, not just a screenshot. AI rebuilds real code far better than it guesses from a picture.

**2. Write your brand down before you prompt.** Claude can't match your brand if it lives only in your head. My site has a BRAND.md file (colors, fonts, voice, what I never say) and a DESIGN.md file (how those turn into buttons and layouts). If you don't have one yet, start with [how to create a BRAND.md file](/ai/how-to-create-a-brand-md-file). It's the single biggest reason this worked.

**3. Paste the source with one line on top.** In [Claude Code](/ai/how-to-setup-claude-code), I pasted ThreeUI's integration prompt and added one sentence above it:

> "Let's turn my /press page into something like this shelf landing page. Let's use my branding and design but take the aspects of the actual shelf interaction and 3D interaction from below."

That one line tells Claude which parts to keep (the motion) and which to replace (everything visual).

**4. Tell it what your content is.** The original shelf had seven made-up books. Mine needed 36 real press pieces. Claude grouped them into volumes and made every spread one clipping: the article screenshot on the left page, the headline and summary on the right.

![An open press volume in 3D showing a TechCrunch article screenshot on the left page and its headline and summary on the right, next to the volume's description and clickable outlet links](/ai-guides/how-to-create-interactive-3d-website-with-ai-open-book.png "Each spread is one real clipping, with a link to the full piece.")

### Then make it yours

**5. Test it like a visitor, then write one numbered list.** I opened it in my browser, clicked everything, and sent all my notes at once: remove these labels, rename these books, the open pages are too bright to read, never use that mono font again. One list beats twenty single messages because Claude can fix related things together.

**6. Describe the feeling, not the fix.** I didn't know why the pages looked peach and hard to read. I just said "it still seems a bit saturated, I want the correct color tones so I can read it." Claude found the cause (the 3D lights were tinting the paper) and fixed it. You don't need the technical words, just an honest description of what bugs you.

> **Key insight:** Keep the interaction, replace the look. Your brand file is what turns a 3D component into something that's obviously yours.

**7. Check it on your phone.** My first version blocked scrolling on mobile, because the 3D canvas grabbed every swipe. I only noticed because I tried it on a phone. Always test the phone view before you ship.

![The 3D press shelf on a phone screen, showing the yellow Forbes 30 Under 30 book centered on the shelf with an Open volume button below](/ai-guides/how-to-create-interactive-3d-website-with-ai-mobile.png "The same shelf on mobile.")

**8. Keep a plain list underneath for Google and AI search.** Search engines and ChatGPT can't read what's inside a 3D canvas. So under the shelf there's a normal list of every clipping with real links. Visitors get the fun version, search gets the readable one.
