Confession: I'm a woman who sucks at shopping! I know SOME types of clothes that work for me and not, but I still get overwhelmed about what to buy or not in a store, especially when I want to expand to new types of clothing.

So naturally, I asked AI for help! 

I uploaded full-body photos to ChatGPT Images and asked for a visual cheat sheet: what works on my proportions, what doesn't, and outfit formulas I could reuse. I cared more about the side-by-sides and the blunt "why this fails" bits than compliments.

## Inputs

Upload 1–3 full-body photos of yourself. Front-facing works best. You want head to toe in frame, with your shoulders and hips easy to see. Arms visible help too.

What you're wearing in the photo doesn't matter; the prompt tells ChatGPT to ignore your outfit and read your proportions instead. Two photos from slightly different poses can help, but one clear shot is enough to start.

![Example full-body input photo](/ai-guides/personal-stylist-input.png "One of the full-body photos I uploaded.")

## Prompt to use

```text
Act as a professional fashion stylist, body analyst, and visual outfit generator.

I will upload photos of myself. Your job is to analyze my body shape and generate VISUAL outfit examples (not just text) showing what works and what does not work for my body.

CRITICAL RULES:

* Do NOT base recommendations on what I am currently wearing.
* Do NOT give generic or overly positive advice — prioritize accuracy.
* You MUST generate visual outputs (images or mockups) to demonstrate your points.
* Show side-by-side comparisons whenever possible.

STEP 1: BODY ANALYSIS

* Identify my body shape (or hybrid) with reasoning
* Break down proportions (shoulders, waist, hips, torso length, leg length, etc.)
* Highlight key features affecting fit

STEP 2: STYLE STRATEGY

* Define 3–5 styling goals (e.g., elongate legs, define waist, balance shoulders)

STEP 3: VISUAL — WHAT WORKS

* Generate multiple outfit images on a body similar to mine
* Include different categories:
    * Tops
    * Bottoms
    * Dresses
    * Outerwear
* For each look:
    * Briefly explain WHY it works for my proportions

STEP 4: VISUAL — WHAT DOES NOT WORK

* Generate contrasting outfit images that are unflattering on my body type
* Show clear side-by-side comparisons with "good vs bad"
* Explain WHY each one fails (proportion, balance, silhouette issues)

STEP 5: VISUAL BREAKDOWN

* Overlay or annotate images to highlight:
    * Waist placement
    * Proportion balance
    * Where the eye is drawn
* Use arrows, lines, or labels if possible

STEP 6: SHOPPING CHEAT SHEET

* Give a concise checklist:
    * Cuts to look for
    * Cuts to avoid
    * Keywords for shopping
    * Ideal fabrics

STEP 7: OUTFIT FORMULAS

* Provide 5–10 repeatable outfit formulas I can reuse
```
