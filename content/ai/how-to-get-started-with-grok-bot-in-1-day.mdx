Grok Bot is a personal AI assistant that gives each agent a computer, a way to sign into your tools, a team that can talk to each other, and a marketplace of templates you can import. That combo is why it feels like what OpenClaw should have been if it were built for people who don't want to spend a weekend wiring connectors.

Sam Altman should be sweating. Personal assistants like Grok Bot, Instinct, and [Meta Muse](/ai/how-to-set-up-meta-muse-in-30-minutes) are finally easy enough that normal people will actually use them, which is how you get consumer adoption instead of another power-user toy. None of this is sponsored. I just think it's really cool, and I'm still in testing mode figuring out which tool fits which job.

I resisted another agent app. Then the FOMO got me, I tried it, and I liked it because there isn't as much to set up. The honest trade is in a screenshot I keep coming back to: Miguel de Icaza saying Muse is OpenClaw without the developer portals, connector config, and weekly update tax. Peter Steinberger's reply: "And you give up who gets your data." Easy is the point. Easy is also the cost.

## Why is Grok Bot getting the hype?

Four things. A computer that keeps working after you close the laptop, a vault so it can actually log in, bots that can work as a team, and templates you can import instead of inventing an org chart from scratch.

**1. Each agent gets its own computer**

The memes of people leaving a laptop open, or buying a Mac Mini so an agent can keep clicking, are the old version of this. In Grok Bot, each agent has a screen you can watch. It can browse, click, and keep going after you close your laptop.

A virtual machine here just means a computer that lives in the cloud instead of on your desk. You are not babysitting a local process.

![Grok Bot cloud computer showing a Chrome window open to Google, with a Dancing with the Stars tickets tab in the background](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-agent-computer-chrome.png "This is the agent's computer. Chrome is running inside Grok Bot, not on your Mac.")

**2. You can give it logins and payment details through a vault**

This is the part that actually lets it book a flight, shop, or sign into a site for you, instead of dumping another draft into chat. Same category of risk I flagged in the [Meta Muse 30-minute setup](/ai/how-to-set-up-meta-muse-in-30-minutes): a vault is useful, and it is also a live login.

Do your homework. Start with a throwaway account. Keep approval on before it pays. Skip your bank.

> **Key insight:** The thing that makes these assistants usable for normal people is the same thing that should slow you down: they can act, which means they can also act with your data.

**3. The bots work as a team**

You don't have to message every specialist yourself. I talk to one chief-of-staff agent, and it delegates to research and social the way a manager runs a department. If you've used [subagents](/ai/what-are-subagents) in Claude, this is that idea as a chat list of named teammates.

![Grok Bot product page showing a Sales Outbound bot in a group chat with Account Manager and Chief, plus a sidebar of named teammates](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-team-group-chat.png "Grok's own demo of bots passing work in a group chat. This is the team model, not my personal roster.")

**4. You can import other people's agents instead of starting from zero**

The hardest part of agents is not the model. It's knowing what to automate. Grok lets people publish templates, and you can import them. Browse the [Grok Bot marketplace](https://x.ai/bot/marketplace) before you invent a 12-bot org chart you'll ignore by Thursday.

## How do you get started with Grok Bot in one day?

Download it on desktop and phone first. Then do these five. This is still [agentic AI](/ai/chat-based-ai-vs-agentic-ai), work that keeps going after you lock the screen, packaged as a chat app.

**1. Get in, then drag it into Applications**

Grok Bot is in early beta. The get-started screen is a one-week trial (card required, limited usage), Cursor Ultra at $200 a month, or SuperGrok Heavy if you already have it. Cursor Pro and Pro+ still do not include this. A long agent run can burn the trial in one sitting.

![Grok Bot get started screen with a one-week trial, Cursor Ultra at $200 per month, and SuperGrok Heavy included](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-pricing-trial-ultra.png "Trial, Ultra at $200, or SuperGrok Heavy. That is the real door, not a cheaper Cursor plan.")

![macOS Grok Bot installer window with the Grok Bot app icon next to the Applications folder](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-macos-installer.png "Same drag-to-Applications install as any Mac app. Put the iPhone app on too so the same bots follow you.")

**2. Create a chief of staff first**

Think of Grok Bot as a small team, not one mega-chat. The chief of staff is the manager. It can spawn specialists when a job repeats, and it's the one you actually talk to. Mine was hunting facial-spa openings on Booksy while I was doing something else.

![Grok Bot Chief of Staff chat listing nearby spa options with prices, plus a computer screen panel and a Daily goals and todos routine](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-chief-of-staff-spa-search.png "Chief of Staff doing a real errand: comparing spas, with its own screen and a daily routine on the right.")

**3. Watch the computer until the click makes sense**

Once you see that the agent has a browser and a screen, you'll know what to hand it. Research memos stay in Claude. "Go look at this site and come back with times" is a Grok job. My Dancing with the Stars ticket check lives here because it is a website the bot can keep opening.

**4. Connect the plugins that job needs**

Open Plugins and add the apps that bot should touch. Mine already had Gmail, Google Calendar, and Granola. Ask the chief of staff what else to hand over after you connect, instead of connecting everything on day one. Treat a login as available to the roster, not only the bot you meant it for.

![Grok Bot plugins panel with Gmail, Google Calendar, and Granola marked added, plus search categories for payments, inbox, and scheduling](/ai-guides/how-to-get-started-with-grok-bot-in-1-day-plugins-gmail-calendar.png "Connect the few plugins that job needs. Gmail and Calendar are the boring ones that make a chief of staff useful.")

**5. Set one routine on something you already check**

Routines sit on the right side of the bot. Same idea as putting a repeating event on a calendar. You can write it yourself or ask the agent to set it. Mine runs daily goals in the morning. The one I'd tell a friend to copy is: pick a site you already refresh (tickets, a waitlist, a price) and make that the first routine.
