Your customers already talk in Facebook Groups, Instagram comments, DMs, reviews, and competitor ads. Meta Muse is the personal AI agent I would put on those jobs, because it lives inside those apps and keeps working in the background while you run the company.

I run a two-person business. There is no community manager sitting in those threads. Muse just launched. I have not been running these workflows for months. These are the jobs I would actually give it.

## What can business owners do with Meta Muse?

Muse lives in the Muse app, at muse.ai, and in WhatsApp, on US iPhone and Android. It connects to Facebook, Instagram, and third-party apps, and you control that access. It sits inside the two apps where customers already talk, and it runs on a Muse Secure VM, a dedicated computer with its own browser, so it keeps working while you are in a meeting. That is [agentic AI](/ai/chat-based-ai-vs-agentic-ai), and it has to ask before it sends or buys anything.

**1. The Problem Finder**

Meta Muse can scan Facebook Groups and Instagram posts where people mention or allude to the problem a product would solve. It pulls the exact wording buyers use, not the wording in a pitch deck.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Watch these Facebook Groups and Instagram accounts for people describing [the problem my product solves]: [group or account 1], [group or account 2], [group or account 3].
Every [Friday], send me a brief with the exact posts, who said it, and the wording they used.
Flag anything that sounds like they would pay for [product].
Do not comment or message anyone. Ask me before any public action.
```

**2. The Competitor Ad Spy**

Meta Muse can watch competitors' Facebook ads and their Instagram feed, then send a Friday brief on what they are promoting, where messaging shifted, and where they are pushing. Facebook Ad Library is the public catalog of ads currently running on Facebook and Instagram. Competitor ads are watchable with or without Muse. Muse is the agent that watches them so nobody on the team has to click through the library every week.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Every Friday, watch Facebook Ad Library and Instagram for ads from: [competitor page 1], [competitor page 2], [competitor page 3].
Send a brief covering what they are promoting, messaging shifts from last week, and where they are pushing hardest.
Do not change my ads. Do not reply to anything.
Ask me before you take any action.
```

**3. The Review Fire Alarm**

Meta Muse can watch Facebook reviews and Instagram comments for a bad review or an angry comment, ping within the hour, and attach a drafted reply in the brand's voice. An hour is the difference between a handled comment and a screenshot making the rounds.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Watch my Facebook Page reviews and Instagram comments for anything angry, one-star, or reputation-risky.
Within the hour, ping me with the original text, who posted it, and a reply drafted in my voice.
Do not post the reply.
Wait for my approval before sending anything.
```

**4. The Testimonial Harvester**

Meta Muse can collect praise from Facebook and Instagram comments and DMs, draft a permission ask, and turn approved quotes into testimonial creatives for ads. That praise evaporates if nobody is catching it.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Collect praise about [brand] from Facebook comments, Instagram comments, and DMs.
Save the exact wording and who said it.
Draft a short permission ask in my voice so I can request to use it as a testimonial.
Turn approved quotes into ad-ready testimonial copy.
Do not message anyone or publish creatives until I approve.
```

**5. The Group Goldmine**

Meta Muse can monitor niche Facebook Groups where buyers ask for recommendations every day, and alert the second someone asks for exactly what the business sells. Showing up helpful, not spammy, only works if the alert is fast.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Monitor these Facebook Groups for people asking for a recommendation that matches [product category]: [group 1], [group 2], [group 3].
Alert me the moment someone asks for exactly what I sell.
Draft a helpful reply in my voice that answers the question without a hard pitch.
Do not post until I approve.
```

**6. The Collab Matchmaker**

Meta Muse can find creators and businesses whose audiences overlap, using shared followers and people who engage with both, then rank them by fit and draft a partnership pitch. Those are warm targets. The draft can follow the same short-message structure as [Hypermessage](/ai/hypermessage). Nothing sends until you approve.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Find creators and businesses on Facebook and Instagram whose audiences overlap with mine. Look for shared followers and people who engage with both of us.
Rank the top [10] by fit for a partnership around [offer].
Draft a short partnership pitch in my voice for each.
Do not send any DMs until I approve.
```

**7. The Engagement Autopsy**

Meta Muse can pull the last 90 days of Instagram and Facebook data and separate posts that drove money-attention (saves, shares, link taps, and DMs) from posts that only collected vanity likes. Then it builds a content plan around the actual winners. For Instagram-only pattern hunting inside Claude, there is [InstaSights](/ai/instasights).

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Look at my last 90 days of Instagram and Facebook posts.
Rank which posts drove money-attention: saves, shares, link taps, and DMs. Separate those from posts that only got likes.
Build a content plan for the next [4 weeks] that repeats the winners.
Do not publish anything. Ask me before you take any action.
```

**8. The Sentiment Digest**

Meta Muse can send a Monday digest of everything people said about the brand across Facebook and Instagram: praise, complaints, and questions, with the ones that need a personal reply flagged on top. Nobody on the team learns about a problem from a friend's screenshot.

**Prompt to send Muse:**

```
Every Monday, digest everything people said about [brand] across Facebook and Instagram this week: praise, complaints, and questions.
Put anything that needs a personal reply from me at the top.
Include the original post or comment and a draft reply in my voice.
Do not send replies until I approve.
```

> **Key insight:** Facebook Ad Library is public by law. Muse is not why competitor ads are visible. Muse is why a founder is not the one watching them.
