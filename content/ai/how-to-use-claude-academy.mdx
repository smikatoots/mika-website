Claude Academy is Anthropic's free training hub, and the way through it is three stops: **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations**, then **Introduction to Claude Cowork** (or **Claude Code 101**), then **Use cases** filtered to your job. Do not try to finish the catalog.

I opened [Claude Academy](https://academy.claude.com/?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy) to catch up and immediately understood why people bounce. Courses, tutorials, use cases, live webinars with their team, product tracks for Claude.ai, Cowork, Code, Tag, and the API. It looks like a second job.

![Claude Academy homepage with five product tracks: Claude.ai, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, Claude Tag, and Claude Platform](/ai-guides/how-to-use-claude-academy-homepage.png "Claude Academy is Anthropic's free hub: courses, tutorials, use cases, and live webinars in one place.")

I've taken courses here myself. I run Claude every day in a two-person business, and I teach this stuff. This is the path I'd actually follow, not a list of everything that exists.

## Why this matters

Official Anthropic training is one of the few free AI credentials that still means something when someone asks how you learned this. The trap is treating the catalog like homework. You get your time back when you finish one path and use it on work you already have this week, not when you collect another 40 bookmarks.

If you already bookmarked the older Anthropic Academy catalog from my [5 websites for AI courses and certifications](/ai/five-websites-ai-courses-certifications) guide, this is the new front door. Same company, much bigger library, easier to get lost.

The webinar row alone can feel like a full conference calendar. A lot of those sessions are for platform and IT teams (Google Cloud cost control, agents at scale, Microsoft Foundry). Skip them unless the title is literally your problem this month.

![Claude Academy upcoming live webinars, including Claude Code on Google Cloud and Claude in Microsoft Foundry](/ai-guides/how-to-use-claude-academy-live-webinars.png "Live webinars exist, and you can ask their team questions. Most of the calendar is not a marketer's starting point.")

> **Key insight:** Claude Academy is a menu. Order one thing. Finish it. Use it before you order the next.

## Which Claude Academy courses should you take first?

Take them in this order:

1. **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** (the general course, about 4 hours)
2. **Introduction to Claude Cowork** if you are non-technical, or **Claude Code 101** if you want Claude in your files
3. **Use cases filtered to your role**, starting with Marketing if that is your job

That is the whole path. Everything below is how to click it.

## 1. Start with AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations

On the [Claude Academy homepage](https://academy.claude.com/?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy), go to **Learn AI fundamentals with AI Fluency**. You will see versions for pK-12 educators, students, faculty, builders, and more. Take [AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy). It is 14 lessons, one quiz, about 4 hours, and it is the one that transfers to every other course.

![Claude Academy AI Fluency course list, with AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations at the bottom](/ai-guides/how-to-use-claude-academy-ai-fluency-courses.png "Skip the educator and student tracks unless that is your job. Framework and Foundations is the general one.")

What you actually learn is the **4D framework**: Delegation (what you hand to AI), Description (how you ask), Discernment (how you judge the output), and Diligence (what you stay responsible for). That is the missing layer most "here's a prompt" threads never teach.

You do not have to finish all 4 hours in one sitting. Do the framework lessons first. Come back for the deep dives.

If you have not even set Claude up yet, do [How to Setup Claude](/ai/how-to-setup-claude) before you sit through a course about it.

## 2. Then take Introduction to Claude Cowork (or Claude Code 101)

After Fluency, pick **one** product course. Not both.

**[Introduction to Claude Cowork](https://academy.claude.com/courses/introduction-to-claude-cowork?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy)** if you are non-technical and you want to hand off whole tasks and get a finished file, brief, or deck back. Anthropic describes Cowork as learning to hand off work and get polished deliverables, for yourself and your team. That is the right first move for most marketers, operators, and founders. The course is 14 lessons, about 2.5 hours, and it assumes a paid Claude plan with the desktop app.

**[Claude Code 101](https://academy.claude.com/courses/claude-code-101?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy)** if you already want Claude working in a folder, a terminal, or an editor. Despite the name, you do not need to be a developer. You do need to be okay giving it a workspace. The course is 12 lessons, about 1 hour. If that is you, use [How to Setup Claude Code](/ai/how-to-setup-claude-code) alongside it.

![Claude Academy product cards for Claude Cowork and Claude Code side by side](/ai-guides/how-to-use-claude-academy-cowork-vs-code.png "Non-technical? Start with Cowork. Already want Claude in your files? Take Code.")

Claude Tag (Claude in Slack) and Claude Platform (API) can wait. They are real tracks. They are not stop two.

If you want the mental model behind this split, I compared chat tools vs tools that actually do the work in [Chat-based AI vs agentic AI](/ai/chat-based-ai-vs-agentic-ai).

## 3. Then filter Use cases to your actual job

This is the step that makes the catalog usable.

1. Open [All resources](https://academy.claude.com/all?utm_source=mikareyes.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ai-guide&utm_content=how-to-use-claude-academy).
2. Click the **Use cases** tab. Leave Role on All and the list is huge. That is the overwhelm.
3. Open the **Role** dropdown and pick **Marketing** (or Product, Sales, Finance, HR, whatever you actually do).
4. Pick **one** use case that matches a task you already have this week. Campaign analysis, adapting content across platforms, on-brand drafts. Not the coolest demo. The Tuesday task.

![Claude Academy All resources page with the Use cases tab active and the Role filter open on Marketing, Product, Engineering, and other jobs](/ai-guides/how-to-use-claude-academy-use-cases-role-filter.png "All resources, then Use cases, then Role. Marketing is on the list. That is how you shrink the catalog.")

Run that one use case with your real inputs. Then stop. If you want packaged marketing workflows after that, [install free marketing skills for Claude](/ai/how-to-install-free-marketing-skills-claude) so you are not re-explaining the same brief every time.

Claude.com also has a separate [Use Cases library](/ai/what-is-claudes-use-cases-page). Academy use cases are the course-shaped version, with a Role filter. Same idea, different door. Use the one you will actually finish.

## What should you skip?

Skip trying to complete every AI Fluency variant. Skip Tag and Platform until Cowork or Code is in your week. Skip webinars whose titles name a cloud vendor you do not work in. Skip opening 12 tabs of use cases.

The time this buys you is not "I studied Claude." It is one finished workflow you can run again on Thursday, and the hours you did not spend touring a catalog.
