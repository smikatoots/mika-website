Today, I became a U.S. citizen! 🇺🇸

I will always love my home in the Philippines. 🇵🇭 It's the country that raised me and shape me and I’ll always find ways to honor my roots and give back, now and in the future.

But today, I’m also deeply grateful for what America has offered ambitious dreamers me: the opportunity to envision a future bigger than I ever thought possible, the confidence to start & build businesses I'm proud of, and the freedom to chart my own path. 

After years of visa stress, uncertainty, travel anxiety and countless “sorry, we can’t hire you because of your status” conversations… this feels like a weight lifted.

Here’s to every immigrant who dared to build, dream, and hustle their way here.

Today, I celebrate you all as a newly minted U.S. citizen. 🇺🇸
