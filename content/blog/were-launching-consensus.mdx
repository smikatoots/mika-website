We’ve been testing, prototyping & iterating on this baby for a year now! We changed our rules _seeveraaal_ times after many test plays, evolved from _You vs. Me_ to _Tandemonium_ to _Buddy Wars_ & finally _Consensus_, and even started off writing on the back of business cards to test early concepts!


We’re super excited to finally get Consensus out there. We’re very thankful for friends and family for testing the game until they got tired of playing & for providing super valuable feedback to make Consensus the game it is today!
