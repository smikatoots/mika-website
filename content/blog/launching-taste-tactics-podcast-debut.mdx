🚀 It’s launch day! 🚀

After months of recording, editing, and lots of coffee shop Zoom calls…the Taste & Tactics podcast is officially live.

In each episode, I sit down with a builder I admire to unpack one bold launch:
✨ What sparked their unique idea
📈 The distribution tactics that worked or flopped
👀 And the taste that made customers fall in love

I’m excited to share that our first episode is with Jaclyn Pascocello, Founder of Fabrik, a network of member-based community & social spaces to help solve the $460B loneliness crisis.

On this episode, we discuss:
👉 How their “Founding 50” members jump-started the community
👉 How a casual breakfast series turned into a packed events calendar from Week 1
👉 How learnings from Tribeca helped them launch their newest location in Dumbo

I can’t wait to get your thoughts and feedback on this first conversation!
