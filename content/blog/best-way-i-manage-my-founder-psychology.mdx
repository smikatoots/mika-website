How do I manage my psychology as a founder?


Journaling? Often.


Meditating? Occasionally.


Long walks? Sometimes.


The thing that really does the trick for me: **talking to other founder friends.**


Being a founder is a rare, challenging, and yes, privileged position. Few grasp the highs, lows and sheer weight of the journey -- except other founders.


We celebrate each other's wins, trade war stories, and remind each other that no matter how hard a day is, we're never really alone.
