Hi there!


You probably landed here because you saw that I was part of one of these programs:

- [**South Park Commons (SPC) Community Member**](https://www.southparkcommons.com/community)
- [**Inaugural South Park Commons Founder Fellowship**](https://www.southparkcommons.com/founder-fellowship)
- [**Kleiner Perkins Product Fellow**](https://www.kleinerperkins.com/fellows/)
- **Forbes 30 Under 30 (2024)**

If you’re applying to any of them, congratulations! That’s a big, exciting step. 


And if you were thinking of reaching out to ask for advice: I totally get it. I was in your shoes once too, trying to increase my odds, and I would’ve done the same. 


Trust me: I _really_ wish I could personally chat with everyone who reaches out. 


But over time (and especially during application season), the number of requests has grown so much that I just can’t keep up, no matter how much I’d love to. (Ironically, the content I created to help at scale is what made even _more_ people reach out! 😂)


So I created this post as a way to say: _I see you, I’m rooting for you, and I still want to help, just in a more scalable way._ 


Below are resources I’ve made that go deep into how I approached these programs and how you might too.
