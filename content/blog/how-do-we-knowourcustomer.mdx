At Parallax, one of our core values is to "Cultivate trust, humility, and empathy," especially, deep empathy for our customers.


Recently, we've made changes to our rituals to help _**everyone**_ gain a better understanding of our customers and their needs.


We're trying three new things to achieve our goal of getting to #KnowOurCustomer (fintech friends, get it? 😉):

1. **#customer-insights channel** - We created a Slack channel where anyone can dump screenshots of quotes from different chats or raw notes of customer conversations.
