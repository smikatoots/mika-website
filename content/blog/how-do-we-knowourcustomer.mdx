At Parallax, one of our core values is to "Cultivate trust, humility, and empathy," especially, deep empathy for our customers.


Recently, we've made changes to our rituals to help _**everyone**_ gain a better understanding of our customers and their needs.


We're trying three new things to achieve our goal of getting to #KnowOurCustomer (fintech friends, get it? 😉):

1. **#customer-insights channel** - We created a Slack channel where anyone can dump screenshots of quotes from different chats or raw notes of customer conversations.

    ![Untitled.png](/blog-assets/how-do-we-knowourcustomer/000.png)

2. **Weekly review of customer insights** - Every week, the entire team meets and I present a digest of notes from the previous week's customer calls, along with an updated tally of feature requests.

    ![Untitled.png](/blog-assets/how-do-we-knowourcustomer/001.png)

3. **Join user research calls** - Our user research calls are open to all team members. We plan to require each team member to attend at least 1-2 calls per month to start.

What does your team do to develop deeper empathy for your customers? Would love to hear your tips and insights!
