Actionable steps from [Built to Move](https://www.amazon.com/Built-Move-Essential-Habits-Freely/dp/0593534808) that I’d like to incorporate into my routines for a healthier, more mobile life. 


---


### **1. Improve Daily Mobility**

- Practice the **Sit and Rise Test** to assess mobility levels
- Sit cross-legged, in [90-90 position](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=90%2F90%20position&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fi0.wp.com%2Fpost.healthline.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2021%2F06%2F90-90-stretch-yoga-hip-1296x728-header.jpg%3Fw%3D1155%26h%3D1528&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.healthline.com%2Fhealth%2Ffitness%2F90-90-stretch&docid=9URLhZQzv86UPM&tbnid=Tx6_ctWhtXWRuM&vet=12ahUKEwj8oK3TzcuLAxWVMlkFHdOlDqYQM3oECFYQAA..i&w=1155&h=648&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwj8oK3TzcuLAxWVMlkFHdOlDqYQM3oECFYQAA), or with one leg up throughout the day
- Incorporate hip stretches like the [**Couch Stretch**](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=couch%20stretch&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fgeeksonfeet.com%2Fimg%2Fworkouts%2Fcouchstretch.jpg&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fgeeksonfeet.com%2Fworkouts%2Fcouchstretch%2F&docid=KkkM9h0xnpSkGM&tbnid=FXuzdCC-saU2FM&vet=12ahUKEwi5lYXczcuLAxUCNlkFHQoMNRIQM3oECH4QAA..i&w=1080&h=608&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwi5lYXczcuLAxUCNlkFHQoMNRIQM3oECH4QAA) for better hip mobility

### **2. Walk More & Walk Better**

- Aim for **8,000-12,000 steps daily**
- Take **10-minute walks after meals** to improve digestion and glucose levels
- Try **barefoot walking** occasionally for foot strength and balance
- Add weight (rucking) to walks for increased intensity

### **3. Strengthen Breathing & Posture**

- Focus on **nasal (vs. mouth) breathing** for better oxygen efficiency and stress reduction
- Use the [**Breath-Hold Test**](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=breath-hold%20test&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fs3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fcdn.wellbeingmagazine%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2019%2F04%2F26132915%2FBreath-Check-chart.jpg&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwellbeingmagazine.com%2Fthe-breath-connection-the-most-neglected-health-promoting-factor-in-medicine-today%2F&docid=U62e86xNicHp0M&tbnid=fqsnlESbZnh63M&vet=12ahUKEwjCh_T7zcuLAxWAFVkFHQgqLm0QM3oECFwQAA..i&w=614&h=345&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwjCh_T7zcuLAxWAFVkFHQgqLm0QM3oECFwQAA) to assess breathing control
- Incorporate [**Wim Hof breathing**](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=wim%20hof%20breathing&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fi.ytimg.com%2Fvi%2FtybOi4hjZFQ%2Fmaxresdefault.jpg&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtybOi4hjZFQ&docid=Axg7GiWDQZtdGM&tbnid=xItDz4-bJTMs7M&vet=12ahUKEwj769uSzsuLAxU6KVkFHV4YM6sQM3oECB0QAA..i&w=1280&h=720&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwj769uSzsuLAxU6KVkFHV4YM6sQM3oECB0QAA) or similar deep-breathing exercises

### **4. Eat for Longevity & Performance**

- Consume **800 grams (28 oz) of fruits & vegetables** per day
- Target **0.7-1g of protein per pound of body weight**
- Include **fermented, sprouted, and nutrient-dense foods** (e.g., meat on the bone, offal)
- Try the **Frappuccino test** to assess metabolic flexibility
- Experiment with **occasional fasting (e.g., 24-hour fasts)**

### **5. Strength & Balance Training**

- Practice **deep squats** daily for hip and knee health
- Use **jump rope** for balance training (start with 100-200 jumps)
- Improve stability with the[ ](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=one-leg%20balance%20test&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fi.dailymail.co.uk%2F1s%2F2024%2F10%2F23%2F15%2F91192251-13992279-image-a-2_1729693762359.jpg&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dailymail.co.uk%2Fhealth%2Farticle-13992279%2Fability-balance-one-leg-old-bones-biological-age.html&docid=WDYXrY78TyiPTM&tbnid=3BCFzRcox7KIRM&vet=12ahUKEwit_N3d0MuLAxXIFFkFHQx6CxIQM3oECH8QAA..i&w=634&h=891&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwit_N3d0MuLAxXIFFkFHQx6CxIQM3oECH8QAA)[**One-Leg Balance Test**](https://www.google.com/imgres?q=one-leg%20balance%20test&imgurl=https%3A%2F%2Fi.dailymail.co.uk%2F1s%2F2024%2F10%2F23%2F15%2F91192251-13992279-image-a-2_1729693762359.jpg&imgrefurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.dailymail.co.uk%2Fhealth%2Farticle-13992279%2Fability-balance-one-leg-old-bones-biological-age.html&docid=WDYXrY78TyiPTM&tbnid=3BCFzRcox7KIRM&vet=12ahUKEwit_N3d0MuLAxXIFFkFHQx6CxIQM3oECH8QAA..i&w=634&h=891&hcb=2&ved=2ahUKEwit_N3d0MuLAxXIFFkFHQx6CxIQM3oECH8QAA). Do this during teeth brushing

### **6. Reduce Sitting & Increase Active Workspaces**

- Track daily sitting time and aim for **dynamic sitting or standing workstations**
- Avoid sitting for long periods—switch positions frequently

### **7. Prioritize Sleep & Recovery**

- Aim for **7+ hours of quality sleep** per night
- Maintain a **consistent sleep schedule**, even on weekends
- Reduce **screen time before bed** and eliminate electronics in the bedroom
- Sleep in a **cool, dark, and quiet environment**

### **8. Experiment with Recovery Practices**

- Try **sauna therapy (5-20 min sessions)** for immune and recovery benefits
- Use **cold immersion therapy** to reduce inflammation
- Alternate between heat and cold exposure for contrast therapy benefits
