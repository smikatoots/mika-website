I recently fulfilled a bucket list item: get on live TV. 📺


I got the chance to share the goodness of [**Parallax (withparallax.com)**](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#) via 2 top media stations in the country:

- [**ABS-CBN Corporation**](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#)'s Business Roadshow with [**Denice Dinsay - Chua**](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#)
- [**People's Television Network, Inc.**](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#) with [**Charms W. Espina, EnP**](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#)

Not gonna lie, nerves were high up until the very last minute, but that made for a more exciting experience.
