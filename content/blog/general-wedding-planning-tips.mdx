I’m in the midst of wedding planning (as a Type A bride, I’m actually having a ton of fun!). Thought I’d write then an ongoing list of small wedding planning tips as I feel it bubbling. 


Enjoy, brides and grooms to be!

1. **Create a shared wedding email to receive all wedding vendor information.** For example:: nickandmika[at]gmail[dot]com. Label your emails accordingly. You can even use this email beyond the actual wedding!
2. **Prioritize important things, pay cheaply for the rest.** As with everything in life and work, weddings have tradeoffs. The constraint: budget. So it’s important to prioritize.

    For example: We cared more about videography than decorations so we found the cheapest decor package so we could move more of our budget to the videographer.
