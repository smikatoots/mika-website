Excited to announce Nicolas and I are officially launching our first physical product: Desk Grass™ 🌱

The problem was staring us in the face. Every founder, every exec, every knowledge worker has heard the same advice:

"You need to touch grass."

And they're right. 

Studies show that contact with natural greenery reduces cortisol, improves focus, and increases creative output by up to 37%.

But when are you supposed to do that? Between the 9am board prep and the 4pm investor update? During your 23-minute lunch where you're also answering Slack?

You don't have time to go outside. 

So we're bringing the grass to you.

Desk Grass™ is a premium, hand-cultivated patch of real grass engineered to sit right next to your keyboard. Whenever the stress hits, you feel the earth beneath your fingers and efficiently go back to your spreadsheet.

We've been dogfooding this for 3 weeks. Our stress levels are down. Our revenue is... the same. But we FEEL better.
