We vibecoded a Singles website for our wedding! 

One of my fave & unique things we did at our wedding (of which there are many!!) 

1. We asked the single eligibles attending our wedding to fill out a form (on Tally, of course!)

2. We put them up on our a wedding website

3. We gave a mysterious QR code next to their name card saying "You're next!" 

Results: 
- 17 sign ups (11% of our guests)
- 42 unique visitors to the singles website on the day of our wedding
- 101 pageviews to the singles website on the day of our wedding
- 3 dates!!!
