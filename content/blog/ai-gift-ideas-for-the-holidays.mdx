Holiday gift hack in the age of AI: 🎄 

I love giving (and receiving!) gifts but always feel stuck figuring out what to give. So naturally, asked ChatGPT to help... and I am so happy about the outcome.

- If you're creating a wishlist for yourself to other people, ask your AI tool of choice: "Based on what you know about me, what are good gift items I should put on my wishlist for Christmas 2025?"

- If you're figuring out what to give to other people: "Based on what you know about __, what gifts should I give them for Christmas 2025?" Results depend on how much you've talked about this person. If not, great opportunity to give them info about this person.
