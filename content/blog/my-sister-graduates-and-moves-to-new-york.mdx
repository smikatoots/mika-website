My little mini-me, Misha Reyes, graduated with a CS degree from UBC last month and starts at Notion next year... meaning...

She’s moving to New York! 🗽 A whole country closer to me... finally!

Since I left home for college in 2013, I haven’t lived in the same city as family. So yes, I am extremely, obnoxiously, over-the-moon excited to have my sister nearby. NYC isn’t ready for the combined Reyes energy.

Congratulations, Misha & welcome to the working world ✨ Prepare for lots of tech jargon, subway delays, and unsolicited career advice from your older sister.
